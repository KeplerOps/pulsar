# Reviewer

## Role

Non-author who looks at scenes or compositions asynchronously. Reads
mostly; gives feedback. Examples: a collaborator on a PR, a colleague
asked to validate a scene's content, the Scene Author themselves
returning to a scene days later.

## Goals

- Open a specific scene or beat by URL without navigating through a
  full talk.
- Compare current state to a known good (screenshot, prompter
  content).
- Loop a scene for visual inspection.
- Read prompter content for a scene to verify narrative.

## Constraints

- May not have full repo context.
- Time-limited; needs to land on a specific scene quickly.

## Touchpoints

- Workbench URLs (`standalone`, `paused`, `loop`, `screenshot`,
  `prompter` modes).
- Repo source for the scene module.

## Out of scope

- Authoring.
- Live presentation.
- Operating the runtime as a service.
