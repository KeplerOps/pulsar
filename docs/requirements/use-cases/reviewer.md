# Reviewer — Use Cases

| UID | Title |
|-----|-------|
| UC-R1 | Open a specific scene by URL |
| UC-R2 | Step through a scene with scrub controls |
| UC-R3 | Capture a screenshot for comparison |
| UC-R4 | Read prompter content for a scene |
| UC-R5 | Loop a scene for visual inspection |

## UC-R1: Open a specific scene by URL

Reviewer pastes a workbench URL and lands on the exact scene/beat
without manual navigation.

## UC-R2: Step through a scene with scrub controls

Reviewer loads a scene in `mode=scrub`, steps through beats, and
inspects timing or layout.

## UC-R3: Capture a screenshot for comparison

Reviewer loads a scene in `mode=screenshot` and saves the result for
comparison against a known good.

## UC-R4: Read prompter content for a scene

Reviewer loads `mode=prompter` to read captions/scripted content
without rendering visuals.

## UC-R5: Loop a scene for visual inspection

Reviewer loads a scene in `mode=loop` to inspect motion or audio
behavior repeatedly.
